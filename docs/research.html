Scientific publications, conference presentations and the research team behind PEAXAI.

Scientific publications

PEAXAI is grounded in peer-reviewed research. The first article introduces the probabilistic methodology; the second describes the R package that implements it.

  • 2026
    Probability-based efficiency analysis through machine learning
    Ricardo González-Moyano, Juan Aparicio, José L. Zofío, Víctor J. España
    Machine Learning with Applications, 24, 100920
    Methodology and application to 917 Spanish food-industry firms. Open access.
    Read the articleDOI: 10.1016/j.mlwa.2026.100920
  • 2026
    PEAXAI: A probabilistic efficiency analysis using explainable artificial intelligence in R
    Ricardo González-Moyano, Juan Aparicio, José L. Zofío, Víctor J. España
    SoftwareX, 36, 103089
    Software article describing the R package. Open access.
    Read the articleDOI: 10.1016/j.softx.2026.103089
  • In press
    Artificial intelligence methodologies in efficiency and productivity analysis: state of the art
    Ricardo González-Moyano, José L. Zofío, Juan Aparicio
    Chapter 8 in C. Daraio (ed.), Handbook on Research Methods and Applications on Productivity and Efficiency. Edward Elgar
    State-of-the-art review that situates PEAXAI in the literature.
